# kailor product vision

kailor helps people understand which technology changes could improve their work. It starts with their circumstances rather than a preferred technology. Users may be established, starting, uncertain or specific about a requirement.

The first iteration is a private tool for the founder and sole in-house consultant. A subject can represent a business, independent professional or individual. Each discovery has a stable identity, versioned definition and backend-owned state. An external customer interface or mobile client can later consume the same services with a separate authorization boundary.

The journey is introduction → guided discovery ↔ conversation ↔ solution canvas → assessment → interactive analysis → PDF. The interface is calm, light and editorial. The future mascot has a configurable component; no replacement character has been invented.

Success means relevant questions, preserved confirmed context, proportionate conditional recommendations, honest uncertainty and useful next steps. It does not mean maximizing software scope or recommending AI everywhere. No commercial outcomes, ROI, market prices or binding quotations are invented.

Out of scope: customer dashboards, payment processing, CRM, marketplace, recurring kai publication, native mobile delivery, verified client success claims and automatic learning from private records.
